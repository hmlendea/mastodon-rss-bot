## 🎯 Purpose

This project aims to be a constructive, welcoming place for everyone who participates in it. This code explains the conduct expected in project spaces and the manner in which concerns are managed.

## 🤝 Expected Conduct

Participants are expected to:
* Treat other people with courtesy, patience, and consideration.
* Employ clear, inclusive language and accommodate different perspectives.
* Provide and receive feedback regarding ideas and efforts without attacking people.
* Maintain focus on the project in discussions while recognising the people involved.
* Take responsibility for mistakes and assist in repairing their impact.

## 🚫 Unacceptable Conduct

The following conduct is not acceptable in project spaces:
* Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or targeted hostility.
* Threats, unwanted sexual attention, or sexualised language or imagery.
* Personal attacks, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith provocation.
* Sharing someone else's private or identifying information without authorisation.
* Retaliating against someone who raises a concern or participates in a review.
* Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider unsafe or seriously disruptive to a professional community.

## 🛡️ Participation and Moderation

Maintainers are responsible for applying this code consistently and proportionately. They may pause a discussion, remove material, restrict participation, or take other corrective action when necessary to protect the community and maintain the project's functioning.

Moderation decisions should be explained where doing so does not compromise privacy, safety, or a continuing review. Maintainers should provide people with a reasonable opportunity to clarify or correct conduct when circumstances permit.

## 🌐 Where It Applies

This code applies to repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, chat spaces, events, and other spaces operated for the project. It also applies when someone is visibly representing the project or acting in an official project role in another location.

## 📣 Reporting Concerns

Report conduct concerns to the repository maintainers. Provide sufficient context for the maintainers to comprehend what happened, when it happened, where it happened, and what response you are seeking. Do not include information that is unnecessary for reviewing the concern.

Reports will be treated respectfully and shared only with people who require the information to review or respond. Confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where disclosure is required to protect someone or comply with legislation.

## ⚖️ Response and Enforcement

Maintainers will acknowledge reports when practicable, assess them fairly, and provide a proportionate response. Possible responses include clarification, mediation, a request to cease particular conduct, removal of content, temporary restrictions, or permanent removal from project spaces.

People who make a report in good faith will not be penalised for reporting. A report that is knowingly false, threatening, or retaliatory may itself violate this code.
